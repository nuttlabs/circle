## Living workshops.

### Evolve with Notion.

### Follow core principles.

Mastering Notion starts with your mindset. Through his work with users, collaborations with the Notion team, and countless hours of tinkering in his own workspaces, William established a set of principles for learning and applying Notion.

Those principles will underpin every Notion A-to-Z workshop, and William will review them frequently to keep them front of mind.

### Master the fundamentals.

To make a great cake, you need to understand your kitchen tools and the role of each ingredient. An intelligent Notion system works the same way. Implementing strategies and frameworks requires tactical skill with Notion’s building blocks.

Throughout his demonstrations, William will explain his actions so members understand the role of each feature at every step.

### Build Bulletproof systems.

Members will have exclusive access to the upcoming version of William’s acclaimed Bulletproof framework, which will infuse Notion’s latest features and, for the first time, include adaptations for other database and spreadsheet apps, including Airtable and Google Sheets.

In Notion A-to-Z workshops, William will rebuild components and demonstrate how to expand the system with new master databases, including many he uses in his own workflow.

Along the way, he’ll reinforce his core principles and Notion fundamentals until they click, giving members the foundation to apply them for their unique needs.

### Automate your workflow.

Databases create the structure needed for automation, the second pillar of William’s Productivity Nexus.

An intelligent Notion system is flush with green flags for automation, whether through database automations, buttons, or custom agents. Using his own workflows, William will demonstrate how to identify and implement automation opportunities and deliver regular updates on these quickly evolving capabilities.

### Integrate with other apps.

Done right, Notion is the hub of your software ecosystem, continuously exchanging information with other apps to establish a true system of record and eliminate redundancy. That's achieved through integrations, which typically pair with the triggers and actions of automations.

William will showcase his favorites, along with the almighty webhook.

### Orchestrate agents.

Notion began as a one-stop shop for managing information and processes. With the addition of agents, it is also becoming a central destination for AI, with access to top frontier models.

Agents can help build your system, populate content, and reference information across your workspace, the web, and other apps. They are essential to a modern workflow, and William will show you how to get the most from them.

### Keep pace with Notion.

Notion is evolving at light speed. The reimagined format of Notion A-to-Z is designed to stay current as the team adds new features and novel strategies emerge. William will regularly recap those features and demonstrate how to incorporate them into your existing systems.

### Tailor the lessons.

Submit your questions, topic requests, and use cases. Notion AI will surface common themes, helping William prioritize future workshops.

### Practice through problem-solving.

Among William’s core principles is to “be a builder, not a user.” There’s no better way to learn Notion than working through real challenges. William will often assign exercises to help new concepts click.

### Tune in on your time.

Workshops will be available on demand in a library filterable by feature, function, date, and other useful classifications. Each will include an AI summary and timestamps.
