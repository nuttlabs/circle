Databases create the structure needed for automation, the second pillar of William’s Productivity Nexus.

An intelligent Notion system is flush with green flags for automation, whether through database automations, buttons, or Workers, or AI Agents. Using his own workflows, William will demonstrate how to identify and implement automation opportunities and deliver regular updates on these quickly evolving capabilities.

Done right, Notion is the hub of your software ecosystem, continuously exchanging information with other apps to establish a true system of record and eliminate redundancy. That's achieved through integrations, which typically pair with the triggers and actions of automations.

Notion began as a one-stop shop for managing information and processes. With the addition of Workers and Custom Agents, it has become the hub of your information ecosystem and a command center for interoperable AI.

Workers and Custom Agents can help build your system, enrich content, sync with external sources, communicate with other apps and agents, and answer questions about your information from wherever you work. They are essential to a modern workflow, and William will show you how to get the most from them.
